## Measured conclusion

Fusion improves mean test AP over VGAE on each dataset: Cora 88.97% versus 84.43%, CiteSeer 85.60% versus 80.20%, and PubMed 94.27% versus 93.32%. GAE has the highest mean AP among the main methods on all three datasets. These are balanced, static, transductive test-pair results; they do not establish temporal forecasting or population probability calibration.
